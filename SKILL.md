# YouTube channel standard

## Check the item type before touching the thumbnail

- Confirm whether the item is a Short or a regular video before you generate, pick, or upload any thumbnail.

- Give Shorts a vertical 9:16 thumbnail (1080x1920) and regular videos a landscape 16:9 thumbnail (1280x720). Never cross the two.

- Do the work in the existing logged-in YouTube Studio tab. Do not open a fresh login tab for it.

## Apply the locked channel standard

- Set Category to Education and video language to Hindi on every item.

- Take Hindi plus English captions only from YouTube's real auto-captions or auto-translate. Never invent or fake a transcript.

- Leave captions off and report the item when auto-captions are unavailable.

## Make thumbnails bold and high-CTR

- Use big readable text, a clear hook, and a layout that stays legible on a phone screen. Plain thumbnails get sent back.

- Generate the thumbnail in the correct orientation first and show it before uploading, so a format mistake is caught before it reaches the channel.

## Leave blocked items alone

- Leave the item as-is and report it when Studio refuses Details saves over a pre-existing oversized tag chip. Do not trim tags or force the save. The user decides.

- Report missing English auto-translate captions instead of working around them.

## Confirm with a short card

- Report one short card per item with the title, the link, and what was set: thumbnail orientation, category, language, captions, and visibility.

- Apply the standard in the same pass as the upload, so no item goes live without it.
